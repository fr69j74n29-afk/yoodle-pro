Questions HVAC technicians ask

Does it cost anything to join?

Joining and listing your business is free. There's no bidding, no lead fees and no commission on jobs you win.

Do I need a licence?

You can join now. Add your licence number and Yoodle checks it, then your profile shows a Verified licence badge. A licence may be required to keep a HVAC Pro profile active in the future.

How do homeowners contact me?

They find your pin on the Yoodle map or your company page, then message you through Yoodle. Your phone number and email are never shown on your public page.

Which areas does Yoodle cover?

Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. You set your service radius when you create your Pro profile.
